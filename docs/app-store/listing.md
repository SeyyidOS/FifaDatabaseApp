## English (primary)

**Subtitle** (30 max)

**Keywords** (100 max, comma separated, no spaces)

**Promotional text** (170 max; can change without review)

**Description** (4000 max)

    Matchnight is a match-night logbook for groups of friends who play football games together. Who played with whom, who won, who was king of the night: all of it kept, all in one place.

    START YOUR LEAGUE
    • Open a league and invite your friends: a personal link is for one player; with the group link, people pick who they are and you confirm it.
    • Leagues are private; nobody sees one without an invite.

    LOG YOUR MATCHES
    • One on one or two on two; the clubs each side played and the score.
    • Start a match on one device and enter the result on another: everyone sees what's being played now.
    • Fix or take back a wrong score within 15 minutes.

    RATINGS AND TABLES
    • An Elo rating for every player: beating a stronger side earns more, and the clubs' strength counts too.
    • League table, power rankings, form and player profiles: best partner, favourite victim, nemesis.
    • Compare two players or two duos head to head.

    FAIR TEAMS
    • Draw balanced teams and clubs; the odds show before kick-off.

    NIGHT POST
    • A recap of every match night: score of the night, king of the night, upsets and how the table moved.

    STATS PHOTOS
    • Add a photo of the match summary screen if you like. You are asked first: with your OK an AI service reads the stats; without it the photo is only kept.

    PRIVACY
    • No passwords: you sign in with a code sent to your e-mail.
    • No ads, analytics or tracking. You can delete your account inside the app.

    Matchnight is an independent app, not affiliated with any game publisher or football club.
